Isshoikka
Ikebana · One Life, One Flower
Zen and flower, one taste: in the nature of a flower, we meet the nature of life.

Isshoikka began in 2015 to bring Zen into the practice of ikebana — the flower as a mirror in which we see ourselves. It now has 260,000 followers, and its workshops in Silicon Valley are warmly received.

Its founder, Xuan Guang, has practiced ikebana for more than a decade, studying and exhibiting many times at the Ikenobo headquarters in Kyoto, and holds the Ikenobo teaching license. Zen practice flows into every arrangement, opening a deep and quiet joy of being one with nature. Carried into daily life, this same “Zen and flower, one taste” becomes more ease, more coherence, more naturalness.

Falling Stars
Poetry · Jiuge Poetry Society
Stars fall to earth as words; words, refined, return as stars.

For more than twenty years Xuan Guang has written poetry whenever something moved the heart — the turning seasons, seas becoming fields, mountains and rivers, grasses and flowers, sun, moon and stars. Not polished for its own sake, but written as the heart goes, in conversation with heaven and earth, often reaching beyond the dust of the world. Recent poems carry Zen into verse, tracing back to the source.

Over a thousand poems, collected in Falling Stars.

In 2022, co-founded the Jiuge Poetry Society at Google — gatherings online and in person, introductions to classical poetry, and the sharing of new work — reaching more than a thousand people.

Taixuan Guqin Society
Music · Guqin
Eyes follow the returning geese, hands sweep the five strings; at ease, the mind roams the great mystery.

The Taixuan Guqin Society began in 2022, to find in the sound of the qin the unity of heaven and humankind. We prize the plain over the ornate — music that is natural and unforced. In playing, we watch the movement of feeling within, and become one with nature.

The society has performed widely at cultural gatherings across Silicon Valley and is invited each year by the City of Mountain View for its Lunar New Year celebration. It now has more than a hundred members.

Lumilab.AI
Technology
Frontier AI meets traditional wisdom, bringing inner exploration into daily life — so that each of us can be the master of the present moment.

Lumilab.AI was founded in 2025 to join the latest AI with traditional wisdom — helping people understand their inner system, above all to be aware of and to regulate their emotions, and to build a steadier, more coherent and happier life.

06 · Design & Making
Collected Objects
Objects · Antiques · Tea Ware · Vessels · Censers · Scrolls
Beautiful souls outlast time; across a thousand years they bear witness to the permanence of life.

Objects are witnesses to seas turning into fields. Their makers are gone, yet their spirit and feeling flow on through what they made, still inspiring people a thousand years later. Look closely at what endures through inheritance, and you glimpse the permanence of life.

Starlia collects antiques rich in human feeling and warmth — tea ware, flower vessels, porcelain, ironware, censers, scrolls — and from these witnesses of time sows the seed of searching for what is imperishable within.

Zen · Practice

In 2023, by a turn of fate, Xuan Guang set out on the path of Zen practice. Everything learned before fell into place, and the direction of life became clear: to explore that perfect and complete inner source.

Starlia holds this: the essence of life is transcendent wisdom, and it shows itself in every good quality. When we bring awareness, everything becomes a path toward that beautiful essence. May everyone walk this path of light.

Xuan Guang
Li Qian

Xuan Guang, born Li Qian, has family roots in Hangzhou and lives in Silicon Valley. Bachelor’s degrees from Zhejiang University and the University of Hong Kong; master’s and PhD in computer science from the University of Michigan. More than ten years at Facebook and Google as an engineering manager.

Raised in a family steeped in the arts, with interests spanning poetry, music, ikebana, tea, kendo, ceramics, woodwork, hanfu and collecting. Founder of Isshoikka, the Taixuan Guqin Society, the Jiuge Poetry Society, Ocean & Morus, Starlia, Lumilab.AI and the Inner Exploration Lab.

Many years of sustained Zen practice and volunteer work, devoted to bringing inner exploration into every field — to inspire a life that is whole, complete, happy and joyful.
